# WoWAnalyzer audit: Elemental Shaman

- **Clone:** WoWAnalyzer `0ffe84575780ee3d3fae9ef29f0791dd135d0fc9`
  (branch `midnight`, 2026-10-13), read 2026-10-16. Records extracted by
  Sonnet subagents (Shaman shared layer, then the spec), spot-checked
  (Flame Shock refresh / uptime / Lava Burst count, Chain Lightning
  targets, Maelstrom waste tiers, cast-efficiency targets: 6 of 6
  matched); reconciled in the main session. Pilot spec of the WoW audit.
  Reference only (AGPL): our own words, no ids or tables taken.
- **Spec:** declares 12.1.0, fully maintained; last change 2026-09-18
  (Call of the Ancestors section). No priority list and no checklist:
  every grade is a guide section. Most modules have no changelog entry of
  their own, so their patch is the 2025-12-17 "Updated for Midnight".
- **App:** `lib/damage/wow/specs/ranged.ts` (`"Elemental Shaman"`:
  Ascendance window, Maelstrom at the cap, Flame Shock uptime),
  `tracked-cooldowns.ts` (Ascendance), `potion.ts`, generic checks.
  Case study: qpZx3mWaG7gcDdYB pull 3 (Cocroach, 30th percentile) against
  JZjf9x3mK76NzabP fight 9 (Nickshockz, 99th), both pages saved; the
  header of ranged.ts records what matched.
- **Phase 3 (2026-10-16):** measured on Cocroach's 11 Elemental pulls of
  gVDPhzp61Zm29HMw (2026-10-13; he played Enhancement on the other 5)
  and seven top Mythic parses (Entombed Sentinels: Vr8dLPtFYWA4G1yg
  fight 39 Malegusxd, baRCnPv62YQ1dJZN fight 32 Fauni, JvQpZk3CXj78Dx2f
  fight 67 Kullingen and Spongêbob; Vashnik the Malignant:
  r9CX1kpRGLmKJH4M fight 56 Elemeta and Unlucky, 9w3bK4qhrRC82gVX fight
  29 Shaziggla). Every player had Master of the Elements, Farseer,
  Elemental Blast, Stormkeeper and Ascendance; Power of the Maelstrom on
  some. The app's checks gave the top seven no Ascendance, Maelstrom or
  Flame Shock finding (one 8k Ascendance window on Shaziggla) and fired
  on Cocroach (potions, Flame Shock 18.2s at Coiled Altar 2:56, short
  Ascendance windows). The first pass marked five candidates `wrong`
  because the top players did the same as often or more, which is weak
  evidence on its own (top players also lose damage). **Re-measured
  2026-10-16 as damage lost** on the same pulls (Cocroach's 12 Elemental
  pulls, counting a 0:52 Kith'ix wipe): one-target Chain Lightning, Lava
  Burst without Flame Shock, non-overload casts in Ascendance and
  unbuffed spenders are real losses, top players included, and are
  candidates. Early Flame Shock refreshes are a real but small loss
  (5-62k a pull here) and are a low-value candidate too.
- **Core rules** (active time, cast efficiency, potions, cancelled casts)
  are in [core.md](core.md); rows below cover only what Elemental sets or
  adds.

## Candidates left

The six measured candidates were built 2026-10-16 in
`lib/damage/wow/specs/elemental.ts` (its header holds each model). On
Cocroach's 12 pulls they find, by value: idle with the Ancestors out
15.4M, non-overload casts in Ascendance 1.73M, Lava Burst without Flame
Shock 936k, one-target Chain Lightning 800k, spenders before an instant
Lava Burst 171k, early Flame Shock refreshes 77k. The timeline question
changed three of them while building: Purging Flames pays back about
half of a two-target Voltaic Blaze in Ascendance; Master of the Elements
is never wasted, only spent on a weaker spell; a one-target Chain
Lightning also gets a weaker answer from the Ancestors. Checking the
`no-damage` rows for approval turned up a seventh: the season 2 tier
set's Overcharge, overwritten while up (Cocroach 1.69M over 12 pulls).

Left: **window-spenders**, low value, not measured. The other three
`no-damage` rows are waiting on the user's approval (asked 2026-10-17).

Lesson from the re-measure: value a rule as damage lost first. Top
players doing the same thing is evidence about the premise, not proof
that it costs nothing. The first pass also left out overloads and used
a cast's direct hit only. Measured with scratch scripts, not kept:
`ele-rules2.js` (per player and pull: buffed and unbuffed spenders with
Lava Surge up, the pooled bonus, Flame Shock pandemic clipping with
position from hits taken, Lava Bursts against the player's Flame Shock
debuff, Ascendance window rates and Voltaic Blaze cadence) and
`cl-vs-lb.js`.
